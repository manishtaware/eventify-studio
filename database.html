Entity-Relationship Architecture
users
  • user_id (INT, PK)
  • full_name (VARCHAR)
  • phone_number (VARCHAR, UNIQUE)
  • password_hash (VARCHAR)
  • role (VARCHAR)
  • created_at (TIMESTAMP)
bookings
  • booking_id (INT, PK)
  • user_id (INT, FK → users)
  • booking_ref (VARCHAR, UNIQUE)
  • booking_type (ENUM)
  • item_title (VARCHAR)
  • attendee_name (VARCHAR)
  • attendee_phone (VARCHAR)
  • total_amount (DECIMAL)
  • qr_code_id (VARCHAR)
tickets
  • ticket_id (INT, PK)
  • booking_id (INT, FK → bookings)
  • seat_number (VARCHAR)
  • tier_name (ENUM: VIP/Premium/Gold/Silver)
  • tier_price (DECIMAL)
